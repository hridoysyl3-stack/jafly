Turf Rules & Guidelines

  • Footwear Policy: Metal studs and spikes are strictly prohibited. Only turf boots (TF) or molded rubber studs (AG/FG) are allowed.
  • Arrival Time: Teams must arrive at least 15 minutes before the booked slot to ensure smooth pitch handover.
  • No Smoking/Vaping: Smoking, food, and sugary drinks are strictly not allowed on the turf playing area.
  • Cancellation Policy: Free cancellation up to 24 hours prior to the slot. Late cancellations are subject to review.
